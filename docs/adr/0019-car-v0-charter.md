## The design, one paragraph

CAR v0 is an individual, exposure-sensitive measure over
deduplicated fragile-carrier episodes (one episode, one onset
carrier, per pressure spell — dwell/gap/refractory laws kill frame
farming); continuity resolves tri-state by first terminal event
(horizon expiry is UNKNOWN, never success); each episode's residual
is measured against a leave-player-out corpus-reference rate in a
frozen five-field situation class (region, pressure band,
confinement, support arrival, reception-between-observed-lines via
GEO-1) with a deterministic coarsening ladder; residuals are
interval-propagated through the reinstated field-domain sum; the
replacement reference wears its own tier and a 40+-row Goodhart
table is part of the law. Pressure qualification REUSES the
certified registry primitives — CAR introduces no parallel pressure
thresholds. The episode ledger (CAR-1) ships as a Film Room product
in its own right before any baseline exists.

## THE OWNER FORK (the ratification question)

Does the name CAR denote:
(A) the RESIDUAL SUM — accumulated attributed contribution
    (counsel's recommendation; director concurs), or
(B) the NORMALIZED RATE (per-100-episodes efficiency)?
Whichever loses the name ships as the mandatory companion. Sum
measures how much a player actually added across their real
exposure; rate measures efficiency per opportunity and lets
low-involvement players lead tables.
