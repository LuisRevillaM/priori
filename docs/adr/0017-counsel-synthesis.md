# ADR 0017 — Counsel synthesis: the compiler's next era and the training doctrine v2

Date: 2026-07-10. Status: ACCEPTED (director's ruling on cross-family
counsel). Counsel consulted: delivery/counsel/2026-07-10-sol-papers.md
(GPT-5.6 Sol, fresh instance, read-only exchange; owner-mandated).

## The counsel's headline, accepted as true

"The current system is an unusually rigorous proof harness, but it is
not yet a general compiler... certification covers the middle of the
chain; the two dangerous ends — language-to-intent and pixels-to-world
— remain uncertified." And: "the real moat is not an honest compiler
plus trained vision, but a compiler that tells the vision program
exactly what evidence is economically worth improving."

## Rulings

R-0017-1 (ADOPTED, next-era opener): **Move 1 — the external-
correspondence evaluation system** (sentence→IR contrast sets, IR→plan
equivalence tests, plan→ground-truth fidelity, selective-risk curves,
dev/sealed/rotating panels with leave-domain splits). Subsumes CAP-1's
paraphrase-family design. Sequenced BEFORE any compiler rewrite, per
the counsel's own warning: a new compiler without an independent
semantic oracle merely makes wrong meanings compile more elegantly.

R-0017-2 (ADOPTED as the post-Gate-1 era, SCP-3): **Move 2 — one
canonical CoachQueryIR** (typed temporal relational algebra;
deterministic elaboration; proof-carrying composition refinements on
relation types replacing boolean law; bounded search demoted to
offline discovery). The owner's standing posture — the runtime is
disposable — explicitly invites this. The three-proof decomposition
(parse fidelity / program correctness / empirical validity) becomes
the era's vocabulary; current outputs are renamed honestly ("bound
deterministic answers") until all three certificates exist.

R-0017-3 (ADOPTED directionally, Gate-2-aligned): **Move 3 —
versioned MatchFactStore** with immutable observation manifests
(footage hash, decoder/calibration/tracking/model versions, coverage,
rights) — INT-0 builds toward this shape from day one rather than
retrofitting. Move 5 (the "forever" service: tenancy, snapshots,
saved definitions, semantic migrations) phases into Gate 3.

R-0017-4 (ADOPTED, design law for the next era): **Move 4 — the
provenance-bearing evidence algebra.** Correlated UNKNOWNs sharing a
cause (one missing ball segment) must not double-widen intervals;
internal uncertainty carries provenance tokens and observation tiers,
PROJECTED to PASS/FAIL/UNKNOWN at claim boundaries. Tri-state remains
the public law — it was never meant to be the only internal math.

R-0017-5 (ADOPTED, replaces the doctrine's north star): the
**lexicographic objective** — (1) selective correctness under a frozen
risk ceiling incl. worst-domain groups, (2) predeclared usefulness
floor, (3) information gain (interval width), (4) total economics
incl. annotation minutes. Summary: verified useful information gain
per euro, subject to fixed risk and usefulness floors — scorecard
always visible. Interval attribution must be CAUSAL (which evidence
tokens widened this answer) before it drives allocation.

R-0017-6 (ADOPTED, doctrine ladder reshaped): serial T-rungs become
**parallel D-lanes** — D-0 product/eval contract; D-1 rights-clean
corpus + annotation constitution (label ontology, IAA, blind gold,
deletion lineage); D-2A scene geometry FIRST (calibration is
substrate: "register then track"; hybrid learned-perception +
explicit-camera-model, not another end-to-end keypoint net); D-2B
shared visual backbone (player/GK/referee/ball candidates — player
detection gets its own taxonomy, a gap the draft missed); D-3
temporal game-state inference (tracking + ball + POSSESSION jointly —
the draft's biggest miss: a perfect ball detector still leaves the
flagship vacuous if possession state is wrong; production association
as inspectable factor-graph/min-cost-flow with learned edge scores,
which preserves competing identities and can SAY why continuity is
UNKNOWN — the honesty doctrine extended into the tracker).

R-0017-7 (ADOPTED, strategic): **broadcast and club-tactical footage
are separate domain profiles with separate fidelity tables.** The
first paying substrate is Veo/Hudl-class tactical film — steadier,
wider, fewer cuts — and the training era targets the CUSTOMER
distribution first, not the hardest benchmark. This materially
de-risks Gate 2.

R-0017-8 (ADOPTED with the counsel's corrections): synthetic data
ratings revised (pitch renders strong; jersey composites modest; ball
physics sim as inference prior and test generator, not detector
training), the rights nuance recorded (composites over real crops
inherit the crop's rights; only fully-synthetic scenes are clean by
construction; SkillCorner supplies canonical tracking, NOT pixel-
training rights until proven), and synthetic earns its place by
ablation (real-only vs synthetic-only vs both at matched label
budgets). The flywheel adopts the corrected 8-step loop (attribution
→ candidate formation beyond uncertainty → value-per-minute batch
selection → structured clip correction with blind gold → guarded
teacher-student training → untouched + rotating eval → canary +
auto-rollback → consent/provenance/deletion).

R-0017-9 (DEFERRED, recorded): the serving-runtime industrialization
list (admission control, durable job queue, cooperative cancellation,
shared read-optimized state, async retrieval) is Gate-2/3 work; the
uncancelled-timeout defect is REAL and ships as a near-term fix
packet. The multi-sport ontology-module question is recorded for the
IR design (sport-neutral temporal relational algebra core) but no
sport beyond soccer enters scope.

## Consequences

PLAN gains: SCP-3 era (Moves 1→2) after Gate 1; Gate 2 absorbs
MatchFactStore-shaped INT-0 and the tactical-footage-first profile;
the training doctrine document is superseded by v2 carrying these
rulings; CAP-1 is absorbed into Move 1's contrast-set system; a
near-term fix packet for the uncancelled ask timeout.
