# ADR 0021 — The promise, audited; the Analyst, designed; the Tier Law

Date: 2026-07-18. Status: ACCEPTED (director's ruling under the
engine-run mandate; the owner raised the question and receives the
verdict). Provenance: director position sealed (hash 6909d820),
counsel independent (delivery/counsel/2026-07-18-promise1-*) —
genuinely convergent; the counsel's tier ladder and phasing adopted.

## The verdict (joint)

QUALIFIED YES for retrieval: the ENGINE RUN as sequenced delivers
natural language → certified queries over observable moments,
bounded sequences, geometric shapes, and supported actions. NO for
the expanded analyst promise without designed additions: soccer
analysis is not query compilation, and the coach's tenth question
(conversational reference, saved meanings, contrast construction,
causal diagnosis) defeats any pure retrieval stack. The product's
danger is mistaking retrieval for analysis.

## The shippable promise (product positioning, owner's to amend)

"Ask soccer questions in natural language; the system compiles the
observable parts into inspectable spatiotemporal queries, shows the
exact moments and sequences it found, and helps investigate what
they may mean — clearly separating certified evidence from analyst
interpretation and coaching hypotheses."

## Adopted NOW (join the ENGINE RUN as ship criteria)

E8. SPAN DOSSIER CONTRACT: every match-producing query emits the
    standard span dossier (boundaries+context, identities, certified
    facts and trajectories, provenance/coverage, query-membership
    explanation, render/clip refs, contrast eligibility). The
    primitives are the perception system; the same artifact that
    explains a claim to a human is the analyst's future senses.
E9. THE TIER LAW (constitution-grade): T0 Source Observation →
    T1 Certified Derived Claim → T2 Certified Descriptive Synthesis
    → T3 Agent Interpretation → T4 Hypothesis/Recommendation →
    T5 Unsupported (must not ship). Certification crosses tiers only
    through registered deterministic claims. No prose, visual, or
    recommendation laundering. SENTENCE-LEVEL attribution in mixed
    text. Disagreement between tiers is preserved, never silently
    resolved.
M1+. Move 1's oracle scope EXPANDS: conversational references,
    ambiguity/clarification, threshold grounding, open-question →
    investigation-plan fidelity, evidence → correctly-tiered prose,
    adversarial tenth-questions.

## Recorded for POST-IR (not in the run's ship gate)

Analyst v0 (bounded read-only investigator: hypothesis trees, capped
queries, contrast populations, full search-path disclosure, declared
stopping, tiered findings); Hybrid Span Viewer (dossier + pitch
animation + scoped coordinate tools; source video when
MatchFactStore + rights allow); Saved Investigation Definitions;
the separate analyst scorecard (never collapsed into the compiler's
three-proof certificate).
