# EXAM-1: The Director's Exam

**Author and executor: the director** (keystone work per the
director-loop §0; the owner's insight made it a formal stage: the
director is the same kind of instrument as Hermes and attacks the
engine as a fellow AI before the owner's sealed set runs).
**Review**: the director's code and findings receive the standard
adversarial review — nobody self-certifies, including the director.
**Evidence law**: R-AZ applies to the examiner too — every exhibit is
produced by a committed script, self-stamped, never overwritten.

## The three examinations

### E1. Threshold sensitivity sweeps
The engine's flagship numbers depend on elected constants (carry ≥3.0m,
5.0s/4.0s windows, retention 4.0s within 8.0s, pressure thresholds).
For each: sweep the declared parameter across a grid through the REAL
synthesize→certify→execute path (the node cache makes this cheap —
upstream primitives hit, only threshold-bearing nodes recompute), and
publish the sensitivity curve with honest intervals at every point.
Questions each curve must answer: does the headline number sit on a
plateau or a cliff? Where does UNKNOWN share explode (the measurement
floor)? Is any committed claim an artifact of its constant?

### E2. Adversarial compositions
The director composes meaning expressions the fixtures never
anticipated — deep operator nesting, cross-perspective compositions,
populations at the edge of the subset law, degenerate windows,
zero-width grids — and grades the engine's honesty as much as its
answers: every outcome must be a certified result or a typed refusal
naming the smallest missing thing; any crash, silent coercion, or
wrong-but-confident answer is a finding.

### E3. Honesty edge probes
Directed probes at the places dishonesty historically hides:
UNKNOWN-dominant populations rendered as headlines; interval collapse
at unknown_count=0; refusal gap-code truthfulness on vocabulary
corners; cache-hit answers under mutated upstream data (the Merkle
lineage must miss, never serve stale).

## Deliverables
Committed sweep/probe scripts under scripts/exam/; exhibits under
delivery/packets/exam-1-evidence/runs/ (R-AZ layout); the findings
report EXAM-1-FINDINGS.md ranked by severity, each finding with its
reproduction command. Findings become the final fix packet before
SCP2-E.
