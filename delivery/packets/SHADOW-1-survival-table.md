## Purpose

Before Gate 2 promises "upload your game," measure what actually
survives broadcast-grade tracking TODAY: run vision-derived
coordinates through the real engine and produce the per-question
survival table that PLAN's Gate 2 names.

## Rights posture (hard law)

SoccerNet gamestate-2025 validation sequences are the ONLY available
multi-sequence broadcast source and remain an EVAL-ONLY SANDBOX:
frames and per-frame derived coordinates never leave the vision
workspace, never enter the main repo, never train anything, never
appear in committed overlays or images. What MAY be committed (main
repo, this packet's evidence): aggregate numbers — the survival
table, interval widths, unknown shares, population counts — exactly
as VAL-3b committed GS-HOTA numbers. If counsel doubt arises on any
artifact, STOP and flag.

## Predeclared thresholds (director's act, immovable after first run)

Per question family, on the vision-derived corpus:
- SURVIVES: interval width <= 0.50 AND observed-n >= 10
- MARGINAL: interval width <= 0.90 AND observed-n >= 3
- VACUOUS-ON-THIS-FOOTAGE: everything else
Width = upper - lower on the family's flagship ask; observed-n = rows
with no UNKNOWN component in the joint partition (A+B for rates).

## The five question families (predeclared)

1. Fragile retention rate (the flagship chain).
2. Counterattack sequence rate (flagship 2).
3. Regain counts by zone (aggregate_over count family).
4. What-preceded-X windows (window + trace_back family).
5. Team compactness at regain moments (spatial family — if no
   registered primitive serves it, record NOT-EXPRESSIBLE, which is
   vocabulary demand-evidence, not failure).
