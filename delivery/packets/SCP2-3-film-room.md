## Headline risk

Provenance truth in pixels. The replay must render REAL canonical
tracking frames for each moment's window, with overlays derived from
the ACTUAL evidence rows (witness frames, player ids, statuses) of
the certified plan that answered the question. The failure mode is a
demo that looks alive but draws canned or approximated data — per the
charter, a pixel that is not what its caption says is a defect equal
to a wrong answer. Every rendered moment must be traceable: replay
frames → canonical data; overlays → evidence rows → plan hash.

## Scope

### 1. The surface
A new Film Room route on the existing workshop app service (reuse its
serving infrastructure; do not build a new stack). Components per the
mockup: header with match-scope chips; the ask thread (user turns +
Hermes outcomes); the pitch replay (hero); the answer rail (headline
interval card, partition strip, moments list, provenance strip).
Vanilla or existing-stack frontend — no new heavy framework; the
mockup is plain HTML/CSS/SVG and the product should feel identical.

### 2. The ask path (wire what exists; invent nothing)
Ask bar → `compile_nl_request` (Hermes as built, openai-codex
subscription) → outcome rendering:
- expression → synthesize + certify + execute through the committed
  bridge/search/executor machinery → moments + measurements;
  the compiled chips row shows the operator chain (from the
  expression, not hand-labeled).
- clarification_required → the readings render as choosable buttons;
  the choice resumes the compile (typed state, as built).
- refusals → the named missing capability verbatim from the payload,
  plus the nearest measurable alternative when one exists (propose
  the mechanism; if none exists cleanly, render the refusal alone —
  do not fake nearness).
Every answer carries the provenance strip: plan document_hash, tree.

### 3. The replay
An endpoint serving canonical tracking frames for a moment's window
(both teams + ball, 25Hz, decimation allowed with the rate declared
in the UI); a 2D pitch renderer (SVG/canvas per the mockup's idiom)
with play/pause/scrub, clock + frame readout, and evidence overlays
drawn from the evidence rows: anchor markers, carry trails, stage
labels for sequence chains, per the mockup. UNKNOWN moments render in
slate with their truncation reason (from the chain record).

### 4. Honesty in components
The interval card component takes observed + lower + upper +
unknown_count as REQUIRED constructor inputs — a point estimate
without bounds is unrenderable (the UI enforces the same law as the
data layer). The partition strip and moment list derive from the same
evidence the numbers do.

### 5. Pre-warming and latency
Flagship questions (the fragile-retention baseline and the
counter-attack chain at minimum) pre-warm at startup through a
persistent plan/result cache; the report includes a latency table:
pre-warmed ask, cold ask, replay-frame fetch. True numbers, no
targets.

### 6. Evidence of the loop closing
A scripted end-to-end demonstration (committed): start the service,
ask a flagship question by NL, receive the answer, fetch a moment's
replay frames — assertions on provenance (the answer's plan hash
matches a certified plan; replay frames match canonical data
byte-for-byte for sampled frames). Plus screenshots or a short
recording committed under delivery/packets/scp2-3-evidence/ showing
the surface rendering a real answer (generated by the script against
the real service — provenance-true, per the headline risk).

## Out of scope
Render/production deployment (infra act, director+owner); auth;
multi-user; video footage; any new operators or vocabulary; model
quality work (the capability program is separate).

## Deliverables
The route + components + replay endpoint; pre-warm cache; the
