# Director's sealed position — PROMISE-1: will the stack deliver?
Sealed before counsel. Owner's question verbatim: "whether our
current approach really will deliver the promise of the compelling
language into queries that find in spatial data relevant soccer
moments and sequences and shapes and stuff. And actions." Plus the
agentic vision: open questions ("how could we improve our defense?")
answered by an agent that composes queries AND SEES the spans.

## Position: the approach delivers, with two designed additions

1. Coverage assessment: moments PROVEN; sequences arrive with ALG-8..10;
   shapes with GEO-2/4 + line model; actions with GEO-5/6 +
   event-derived classes. The seven-wall + ALG plan covers the
   "find matching spans" promise. The two REAL gaps:
   (a) the INVESTIGATION layer (multi-query agentic decomposition for
   open questions) — never designed; (b) SPAN PERCEPTION (the LLM
   reading what it found) — never designed.

2. Span perception design: SPAN DOSSIERS — evaluate registered
   primitives over any found span to produce a structured, certified
   percept (shapes, pressures, kinematics, line geometry); the
   primitives ARE the perception system; the LLM sees through
   certified instruments only. PLUS multimodal render reads (Film
   Room frames → vision model) for gestalt, clearly tiered as
   observation-of-rendering, not ground truth.

3. The analyst loop: open question → agent plans queries (existing
   compiler, existing refusals) → runs certified → reads dossiers →
   iterates → BRIEFING: hypotheses as COMMENTARY TIER (visually +
   epistemically distinct from certified claims), every hypothesis
   citing its playlist; the coach's eyes are the final judge.
   Refusals during investigation = vocabulary demand evidence,
   automatically harvested.

4. Sequencing: this is POST-ENGINE-RUN product architecture (Gate 3
   shaped) EXCEPT dossiers, which are cheap and belong in ALG-P's
   explainability packet — the same machinery that explains a claim
   to a human explains a span to an agent.

## Risks I see
- Commentary tier bleeding into certified tier in the UI (the
  gravest possible honesty failure — needs a constitution rule
  BEFORE any agent ships).
- Investigation cost/latency (many queries per open question; needs
  the serving industrialization from R-0017-9).
- Agent cherry-picking spans (needs: briefings must disclose the
  full result sets their hypotheses drew from, not curated subsets).
